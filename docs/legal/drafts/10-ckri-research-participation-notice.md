## 1. What this notice is about

Competen uses what learners do in Scholar to improve nursing education. You can help in two optional ways, and each
is its own choice:
- **collective educational analytics:** your structured learning results are counted together with many others;
- **an identified research study:** you join one named study, which has its own information and ethics approval.

**Both are optional.** Saying no, or changing your mind later, does not change your Scholar learning in any way.

## 2. Four permissions, kept separate

Competen asks for four different permissions. Agreeing to one never means agreeing to another.

| Permission | What it covers | Where you choose |
|---|---|---|
| **Personal AI learning** | AI helps you learn from your own material. | Scholar's AI-processing consent (draft 06). |
| **Collective educational analytics** | Your structured results are counted with others to improve education. | This notice. |
| **Contribution of specific content** | You offer one specific item, such as a case, for a shared library. | Per item. **Not available during the pilot.** |
| **Participation in an identified research study** | You join one named, ethically approved study. | Separately for each study. |

**Your personal AI consent is not research consent.** Turning on AI help in Scholar does not include you in collective
analytics or in any study. [Product: the analytics population is read only from the research choices record, never
from the AI consent. Harness check K5-1.]

## 3. If you choose collective educational analytics

**What is counted.** Only structured results that Scholar already records, such as:
- which competencies your study material maps to, once accepted;
- whether a revision answer was marked correct, and when (by month);
- whether a decision point in a practice case was marked correct, and whether a safety point was raised;
- which competencies you opened learning sessions on, and which library cases you used;
- your programme, level and institution, by their **codes**, at the time.

**What is never collected for analytics or research:**
- your uploaded materials or any text from them;
- your conversations with AI, explanations or generated questions;
- anything you typed in your own words: answers, reasoning, notes, titles, topics, or the names you typed for your
  institution or level;
- AI feedback written for you;
- any clinical or patient details.

**How it is counted.**
- Counting happens inside Competen's database. Individual records are not copied out.
- Results are reported only for groups of at least [OWNER/COUNSEL: the minimum, currently 10, never below 5] people.
- Smaller groups are hidden, and a second group is hidden where needed so the first cannot be worked out.
- Counts are rounded.
- **These steps reduce the risk that someone could be recognised. They do not remove it entirely.**

**Who sees the results.**
- Authorised Competen educators and research governance staff see aggregate results only.
- Nobody outside Competen receives your records.

**What it is used for.**
- Improving learning resources, revision questions, cases and competency frameworks.
- Understanding common learning needs.
- Approved research on nursing education.

**What it is not used for.**
- Grading you.
- Any decision about you.
- Training AI models.
- Anything Scholar shows you.

## 4. If you join an identified research study

Each study:
- is named, with its own purpose, lead and study information;
- has a recorded **ethics approval** before it starts. A research study cannot be exempted from ethics review.
- uses aggregate results only: no one on the study team, inside or outside Competen, receives your individual records;
- shares results with an outside partner only under a signed, authorised agreement that sets out scientific leadership,
  data responsibilities, intellectual property, authorship and publication rights.

You choose each study separately, only while it is recruiting.

## 5. Changing your mind: withdrawal and deletion

- **You can withdraw any choice at any time,** from the same place, with no reason and no effect on your learning.
  Withdrawing always works, even if the research features are otherwise switched off.
- **From then on, you are left out** of every new count and every study you withdrew from.
- **Counts already made** contain no record of you as a person. They are kept until their retention date
  ([OWNER/COUNSEL: currently 365 days by default]), or longer only when an approved study needs them, and then
  deleted. [COUNSEL: confirm this withdrawal effect is adequate, or whether existing counts must be rebuilt without
  the person.]
- **Deleting your Competen account** deletes your research choices and their history.
- Your other rights (access, correction, objection, complaint) are those in the Holdings Privacy Notice (02) §10.
  You may complain to Uganda's Personal Data Protection Office [COUNSEL: confirm the channel].

## 6. Costs and AI

- Taking part never uses your AI allowance and never costs you anything.
- Collective analytics are computed by fixed rules, not by AI.
- Any AI used later to develop shared content is paid for by a platform or institution budget, never by you.
