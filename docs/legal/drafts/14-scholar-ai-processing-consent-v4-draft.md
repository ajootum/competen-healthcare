## Why this is not switched on

**Publishing v4 would ask every v3 learner to agree again, and AI help would stop for them until they did** (`consentStatus` counts a grant only for the current version). The owner's item 2 and item 7 say not to do that for this feature.

**Search History does not need it.** It has its own recorded choice and its own notice (13). v3 stays true with Search History on, because answers are not kept and the history is apart from AI help.

v4 is therefore prepared for the next time the AI notice has to change for its own reasons, or for whenever the owner and counsel decide it should go out. Going live needs a separate owner GO, and the code and `scholar-learning-harness` then change together.
