## 1. About these Terms

1.1 Competen Individual is Competen's personal learning and professional development service for healthcare students and professionals. It has two experiences:
- **Competen Scholar**, for study and preparation for practice;
- **Competen Professional**, for continuing professional development (CPD), development planning, evidence and credentials.

1.2 These Terms are issued by Competen Holdings. They govern:
- (a) the shared Individual foundation: your Competen identity, profile and history, the Competen Passport, and Personal AI;
- (b) Competen Professional, which Competen Holdings operates.

1.3 Competen Scholar is intended to be operated by a separately incorporated subsidiary, the **Scholar operating entity**. Scholar-specific matters are governed by the Competen Scholar Terms of Use issued by that entity. Where you use Scholar, both sets of Terms apply. 1.4 These Terms supplement the Competen Holdings Platform Terms of Use, which govern the shared Competen platform (accounts, authentication, acceptable use). Where these Terms deal with an Individual-specific matter, they apply to that matter.

1.5 **You accept these Terms, and the Privacy Notice, as a pair of versions before you first use Competen Individual.** The version you accepted is recorded with your account (`individual_membership.foundation_terms_version`). If a later version needs acceptance, you are asked again before you continue.

## 2. The pilot

2.1 **Competen Individual is in an invitation-only pilot.** During the pilot:
- (a) access is by **invitation from an authorised Competen administrator** only. Public self-registration is not available;
- (b) **no fees are charged**, and no payment information is collected;
- (c) Personal AI is subject to a usage limit (§9). No AI use is charged to you;
- (d) the shared case library is **not available**. No contribution of your material to any shared library is possible (§10.4);
- (e) features may be added, changed or withdrawn as the pilot develops. Anything shown as "not available yet" or "planned" is not promised.

2.2 An invitation, or pilot access, does not oblige you to buy anything, now or later.

## 3. Eligibility

3.1 Competen Individual is for healthcare students, healthcare professionals and others engaged in relevant education or professional development.

3.2 **Competen Professional** requires an active Professional entitlement. It does not require any previous use of Scholar.

3.3 **Competen Scholar is restricted to people aged 18 or over during the pilot.** When an administrator invites someone to Scholar, the administrator confirms that eligibility, and the confirmation is recorded with the grant. You must not accept a Scholar invitation if you are under 18. See the Adult Eligibility Statement (07).

3.4 You must give accurate information. A confirmation by an administrator does not relieve you of that responsibility. Competen may ask for reasonable verification.

## 4. Invitations, your account and access

4.1 Competen Individual uses your single Competen identity and the shared Competen sign-in.
- **No account yet:** the invitation email lets you set a password and sign in.
- **Existing account:** access is added to it.

4.2 **Nothing is added to your account until you accept the invitation** while signed in as the invited person. Acceptance creates your Individual membership and the invited entitlements. You then accept these Terms and the Privacy Notice before using any experience.

4.3 An invitation can be withdrawn before it is accepted, and it expires on the date stated in it.

4.4 Access is personal. You must not:
- transfer an invitation;
- share your account;
- use another person's identity.

4.5 An entitlement may carry an end date. Competen may suspend or end access to protect security, enforce eligibility or comply with law (§13).

## 5. One identity across your career

5.1 You can hold Scholar and Professional together and switch between them. Adding Professional does not end Scholar.

5.2 Your profile, history and Passport belong to your Competen identity, not to an experience. They remain when an experience ends (§13).

5.3 Continuity does not mean automatic sharing. Information is not shared between experiences, with organisations or with third parties except as these Terms and the Privacy Notices describe.

## 6. Competen Professional

6.1 **What Professional provides:**
- your professional identity record (profession, registration body and number, country, renewal date);
- development needs and goals;
- a private CPD log with reflections;
- self-recorded competency evidence and documents;
- renewal reminders;
- progress views;
- the Competen Passport.

6.2 **Your records are self-declared.** Recording a qualification, registration, CPD activity or competency does not mean Competen has verified it.
- Competen does not check registrations against any register.
- Every record shows its class: self-declared, or document-backed. Neither is labelled "verified".
- You must not misrepresent your qualifications, registration status, scope of practice or competence.

6.3 **CPD.** Recording CPD in Competen does not establish that it counts toward any regulator's or employer's requirement.
- Competen does not hold your regulator's rules or cycles. Hours are shown by calendar year only.
- You remain responsible for meeting the requirements that apply to you.

6.4 **Competency links.** Where Competen offers a competency catalogue, you may link your entries to its competencies. Each offered competency shows its actual clinical review status. A link is your own and is not an assessment. [Pilot note: no catalogue is offered until the owner approves one.]

6.5 **Renewal reminders** are an aid, not an official notification service.
- They are based only on dates you record.
- In-app reminders are on by default. Email reminders are off unless you turn them on.
- Delivery is not guaranteed.

6.6 **No employer access.** Your Professional records are not visible to any employer or organisation through Competen unless you share them yourself (§7).

## 7. The Competen Passport and sharing

7.1 **The Passport shows two kinds of records, kept distinct:**
- records an organisation issued to you, with their issuer;
- records you added yourself, with how they are evidenced.

7.2 **You choose what is shared.** You may create a share link for selected records.
- **Every link expires.** You choose the period, up to the limit Competen sets (no more than one year).
- **A link can be revoked at any time.** Revocation stops further viewing through the link. It cannot recall copies a recipient has already made.
- **You are responsible for who you send a link to.**

7.3 A valid link proves only that the link is valid. Its verification page states, for each record, whether it was issued by an organisation or added by you, and that records you added are not verified.

7.4 You cannot change an organisation's record by its appearing in your Passport. An organisation does not gain access to your private Individual records because it issued a record to you.

## 8. Personal AI

8.1 Personal AI helps with learning and professional development. In Professional, for example, it drafts a CPD plan or a reflection structure for you to edit.

8.2 **AI output may be incomplete or wrong.**
- It is not clinical advice, verification, an assessment or a substitute for supervision.
- Nothing an AI suggests becomes part of your record unless you choose to save it.
- AI must not be used to invent experience, attendance or reflections you did not have.

8.3 **What is and is not retained:**
- Personal AI has no conversational memory between requests.
- Professional AI drafts are shown to you and not saved unless you save them.
- Competen keeps usage metadata (the model, the size of the request, the outcome), not the text of your questions and answers.

8.4 **Patient information.** Before an AI request is made, Competen screens the text for patient identifiers and refuses a request that contains one. Screening cannot catch everything. You must not enter identifiable patient information (§11).

8.5 Competen's AI services are provided through contracted AI providers (the Privacy Notice names them). Your information is not used by Competen to train AI models.

## 9. AI usage limits and fair use

9.1 AI use is limited so the service stays available and fair. **During the pilot the limit is a number of AI requests per hour.** The AI Allowance and Fair-Use Statement (09) explains it.

9.2 When you reach a limit, AI help pauses until the limit resets. **Nothing you have saved is affected**, and every non-AI feature keeps working.

9.3 **You are never charged for AI use during the pilot.** If a measured allowance or paid plans are introduced later, what is included and any charge will be disclosed before it applies to you.

## 10. Your content and intellectual property

10.1 You keep your rights in the material you add.

10.2 **You grant the operating entity a limited, non-exclusive permission** to store, process, display and technically transform your material, only as needed to provide the service you use. This includes sending selected text to an AI provider when you ask for AI help.

10.3 You must have the rights to upload and use what you add (see the Upload Attestation, 08).

10.4 **This permission does not allow Competen to:**
- make your private material public;
- show it to other users;
- use it to train AI models;
- add it to any shared or educational library.

Any future contribution to a shared library would need your separate, explicit, revocable permission for each item, under terms published for it. It is not available in the pilot.

10.5 Competen keeps its rights in its software, designs, trademarks and original content.

## 11. Confidentiality and patient information

11.1 **You must not enter identifiable patient information, or confidential clinical records, anywhere in Competen Individual.** This includes uploads, entries, reflections, evidence, AI requests and documents.

11.2 Automated screening is a safeguard, not a guarantee. You remain responsible for your professional, confidentiality and copyright obligations.

## 12. Availability and changes

12.1 Competen will make reasonable efforts to keep the service available and your retained records intact. Interruptions may occur for maintenance, security events or provider failures.

12.2 Competen may change features. Material changes affecting your rights will be notified and, where required, need your acceptance (§15).

## 13. Suspension, end of access and account closure

13.1 Access to an experience ends when its entitlement ends or is revoked, or when your membership is suspended or revoked.

13.2 When access ends, your shared identity remains.
- Your own Passport records remain readable to you.
- Retained records are handled as the Privacy Notice describes.

13.3 You may ask to close your account or exercise your privacy rights. Some information must be kept for legal compliance, audit or dispute resolution. Competen will not describe a record as deleted merely because it is no longer shown.
