## Two facts that shape the choice

1. **Codes are the stable identifiers; the uuids were generated by the staging seed.** Keeping the uuids too is possible only by inserting with explicit ids, through a seed, never through the framework studio. That matters if anything outside Competen will cite a CF-FOUND uuid (evidence, exports, mappings).
2. **A clinical approval cannot travel.** The 395 content hash covers the competency's name, description, learning outcomes, `expected_level_id`, practice, CPU, risk and skills. Production's level-3 row is a different uuid, so production's hashes will differ even for identical text. Approvals are recorded in production, against production versions, by production reviewers, whatever route is chosen. Staging's review queue is rehearsal, not evidence.

## Option A (recommended where the uuids must match): a reviewed production seed

1. **Write** a production seed for CF-FOUND only:
   - the framework, its 4 domains and 8 competencies with **the staging ids and codes above**;
   - the same names, descriptions and outcomes;
   - `expected_level_id` resolved in production to the default scale's level 3;
   - the framework as **draft**;
   - the S1 "staging seed" wording removed from the framework description. That is content: the staging description reads "…COMPETEN-SCHOLAR-01 S1 staging seed.", and it must not reach production.
2. **Prove it on staging first.** Run it against a staging copy (or a throwaway project) and check: ids and codes match, the content matches field by field, and it is idempotent.
3. **Production GO:** the owner applies it by hand, under the production safeguards (OPS-001). Record the pre-state, check the ids after, and keep rollback SQL (delete by the listed ids while CF-FOUND is still draft and unreferenced).
4. **Publish** CF-FOUND in production through the governed framework lifecycle: submit, then an independent approver. Nothing is published by the seed.
5. **Clinical review:** the curator puts each competency forward (catalogue "review" action, 395). An appointed reviewer with the **CF-FOUND framework scope** decides (first-nursing-reviewer-appointment.md §3).
6. **D1 in production** (D1-production-step.md), only after that and under the activation GO.

## Option B: author it in production through the framework studio

Same content, entered by a curator in production. Codes match; **uuids do not**. No seed, so no seed review, but the content must be checked by hand against the table above. Steps 4 to 6 are the same.

## What is not decided here

- Whether NUR-RESP goes with it, with its four relationships.
- Who the production curator and the production reviewer are.

Each is the owner's decision. This document only makes the routes and their consequences exact.
