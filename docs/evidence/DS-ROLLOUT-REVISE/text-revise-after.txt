Adaptive exam

A practice exam that gets harder or easier as you answer, by a simple stated rule. Formative practice: not an examination.

How it adapts: Harder after 3 correct in a row; easier after 2 of the last 3 missed. The count starts again after each change, and an answer that waits for your own marking at the end does not move it.
